# Male hormones

## Testosterone

### When to test

Total testosterone is measured as part of the standard blood panel for all male members. Note the time of the blood draw in the record.

### Interpreting results

- Below 8 nmol/L: low. Discuss in the debrief and refer to a GP.
- 8–12 nmol/L: borderline. Repeat with a morning sample taken before 11:00.
- Above 12 nmol/L: normal. No action needed.

### Borderline results

If total testosterone is borderline, repeat the test with a morning sample. Until the repeat result is available, give lifestyle advice only and do not discuss treatment.

### Debrief advice

Explain what testosterone does and how lifestyle factors such as sleep, weight, exercise, and alcohol can affect levels.

## Follow-up

Members with low results should be followed up within 6 weeks.
