# Male hormones

## Testosterone

### When to test

Total testosterone is measured as part of the standard blood panel for all male members.

### Interpreting results

- Below 8 nmol/L: low. Discuss in the debrief and refer to a GP.
- 8–12 nmol/L: borderline. Consider a repeat test.
- Above 12 nmol/L: normal. No action needed.

### Borderline results

If total testosterone is borderline, consider a repeat test. The clinician may give advice based on the single result if the member has symptoms.

### Debrief advice

Explain what testosterone does and how lifestyle factors such as sleep, weight, and alcohol can affect levels.

## Follow-up

Members with low results should be followed up within 3 months.
